Explore the Canopy.

From the low-level bamboo thickets to the 60ft high Skywalk, every inch of the park is designed for discovery — and for the pandas who call it home.

Signature Experience

The Skywalk

Walk high among the branches at 60 feet. Our bridges are crafted from transparent sustainable polymer, giving you a glass-floor view of pandas resting in the canopy directly below.

Maximum 6 guests per session ensures an undisturbed experience for both visitors and residents.

Guided Walk

The Bamboo Trail

A 2km loop through our sustainably managed bamboo groves. Walk at ground level with a naturalist guide who can identify individual pandas by coat markings and behaviour.

Morning sessions (7–9am) offer the highest likelihood of active panda sightings before midday rest.

🎋
🏔️
Panoramic Views

The Observation Deck

Perched at 3,200m, the observation deck offers an unobstructed view of the sanctuary's entire canopy system — best experienced at dusk when mist rolls through the valleys.

Complimentary Himalayan tea service available to all deck visitors after 4pm.
