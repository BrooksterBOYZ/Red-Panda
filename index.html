A Perfect Day

Arrive early. Move slowly. Stay for mistfall.

Every visit is intentionally paced, with small groups moving through the sanctuary only when the habitat team clears each route.

Bamboo Trail

Skywalk Window

Small-group canopy observation from the suspended bridge network.
