Our Origin

Born from a shared obsession.

In 2024, all four founders found themselves at the same remote research station in Sikkim, working on unrelated projects. They shared a meal, a flask of tea, and a singular conviction: that red pandas deserved more than chain-link fences and zoo exhibits.

Two years of planning, funding rounds, and government permits later, Velvet Sanctuary opened its doors — a place where conservation funding is woven into every ticket sold.

Our Conservation Work
🏔️

The Founding Principles

  • 01. Animals never perform. Guests observe on the animals' terms.
  • 02. Every material must be local, sustainable, or reclaimed.
  • 03. At least 40% of revenue funds direct conservation efforts.
  • 04. Guest comfort and animal welfare are never in conflict.
