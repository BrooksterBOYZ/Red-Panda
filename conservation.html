Our Approach

Where Your Visit Goes

40% of every ticket sold is directed to three active conservation programs in Sikkim and West Bengal. We publish full financial transparency reports quarterly — because trust is not assumed, it is earned.

🌲

Reforestation Program

Planting native Himalayan species along former logging corridors to restore red panda migration paths.

🏥

Wildlife Rehabilitation

On-site veterinary care for injured and orphaned pandas, with a return-to-wild protocol developed with Oxford University.

🏫

Community Education

Training local farmers in wildlife-compatible land use, reducing human-animal conflict across 12 villages.

🌿

Want to Do More?

Donations to our reforestation fund go directly to planting operations — no administrative overhead deducted.
