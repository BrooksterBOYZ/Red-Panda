The Atmosphere

The café is built from reclaimed Himalayan timber and sits directly beside the canopy observation window — a floor-to-ceiling glass wall that faces the bamboo grove. On quiet mornings, it is not unusual to see a panda perched 10 metres away.

No recorded music. No Wi-Fi. Just altitude, tea, and wildlife.

Chef's Notes

Small kitchen, strict sourcing, mountain timing.

The café menu is built around the sanctuary's garden, nearby farms, and what travels well at altitude. Nothing is served in single-use packaging, and leftovers are composted for the bamboo nursery.

  • 01.
    Morning bake: cardamom buns, millet bread, and sesame shortbread come out before the first trail group returns.
  • 02.
    Tea flight: three small pours matched to weather, from bright first flush to smoky evening blends.
  • 03.
    Quiet tables: the window wall is reservation-only during known panda rest windows.
